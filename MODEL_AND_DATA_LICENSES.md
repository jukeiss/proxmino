# Models and training data: licences and what you must do

Checked 2026-11-05 from each model's and dataset's own Hugging Face page. Not legal advice. Before you sell
Proxmino or sign any agreement, have a parent or guardian look at this with you (contracts need an adult).

Short version: everything Proxmino and Proxmino Mini use allows commercial use, nothing is "non-commercial"
or "share-alike", and the only duties are to keep licence texts and give credit, listed below.

## Web search

The DuckDuckGo fallback (`ddgs`) automates DuckDuckGo's website instead of an official API. It is off in
`config.yaml` (`search.allow_unofficial: false`) and must stay off in anything you publish or sell; your own
local settings currently turn it on, which is fine for personal use only. Brave Search or Tavily keys are the
official route.
